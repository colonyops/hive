**Drive is a tab in Chats too.** The Drive pane beside a chat is gone. An open chat shows as a tab, and the `+` on its tab row opens Drive beside it; split the panel to read a document next to the conversation. A document an agent opens arrives as a Drive tab behind the one you are reading, with a dot until you look at it, and a `hive://chats` link with `canvas=` opens that tab. The title bar's right button no longer toggles Drive in Code or Chats.
