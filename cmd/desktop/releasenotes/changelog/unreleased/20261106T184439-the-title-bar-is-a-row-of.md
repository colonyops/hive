The title bar is a row of tabs. Inbox, Code and Chats stay pinned, and the + menu adds a page: Drive, Tasks, Action runs, Settings, or an applet. Pages stay across restarts, and Tasks and Action runs open as pages instead of over the window. The Views menu is gone. An applet lists the new `page` surface in its `applet.yaml` to open as a page, in no session. ⌘W closes the page in front, including when focus is inside an applet, instead of hiding the window.
