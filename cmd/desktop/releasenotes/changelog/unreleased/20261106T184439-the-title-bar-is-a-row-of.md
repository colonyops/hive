The title bar is a row of tabs. Inbox, Code and Chats stay pinned, and the + menu adds Drive, Tasks, Action runs, Settings or an applet as a tab that stays across restarts. Tasks and Action runs open as tabs instead of over the window, and the Views menu is gone. An applet lists the new `page` surface in its `applet.yaml` to open as a global tab, in no session. ⌘W closes the global tab in front, including when focus is inside an applet, instead of hiding the window.
