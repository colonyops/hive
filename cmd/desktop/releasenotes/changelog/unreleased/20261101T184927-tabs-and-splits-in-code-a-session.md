**Tabs and splits** in Code: a session's windows show as tabs above the terminal, and the `+` on a tab row adds a terminal, an agent, or a Drive, Diff or Tasks tab. Split a panel to the right or downward, nest splits, drag tabs between panels, drag a divider to resize, and turn a split between side by side and stacked. The sidebar lists every tab in the same order, and Hive remembers each session's layout across restarts.
