**Hive Drive has no canvas kind.** `hive drive ls` reports a `.canvas.json` file as a plain `file`, `hive drive prune` no longer keeps `canvases/` folders or `.canvas.json` files, and the `hive dt canvas`, `canvas-block`, `canvas-blocks`, and `canvas-frontmatter` commands and `hive://canvas` links are gone. Use Markdown or HTML documents and `hive://drive/<space>/<path>` links instead.
