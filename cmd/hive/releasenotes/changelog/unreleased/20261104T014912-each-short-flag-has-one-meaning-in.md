**Each short flag has one meaning in every hive command.** The short forms `-a` (`--all`), `-f` (`--force`, `--format`), `-n` (`--new`), `-p` (`--prefix`, `--print`), `-r` (`--repo`), `-s` (`--sender`, `--shared`, `--status`), `-t` (`--tags`, `--title`, `--tool`, `--type`), and `-w` (`--workspace`) no longer exist. Use the long names. `-t` stays `--topic`.
