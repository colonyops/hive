## Fixed

- **Session launch failures show their cause.** The TUI reports the failed pane, exit status, terminal output, and any incomplete cleanup. Successful setup commands leave healthy panes running. When a session's tmux session ends on its own, the TUI says so, except right after an action you ran on that session.
